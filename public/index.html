Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you decide.

How are one-time implementation fees different from the monthly subscription? +

Implementation is paid once upfront so our team can install BOAT, configure your chart of accounts or fee structures, train your staff and optionally migrate your existing records. Your monthly subscription begins once your workspace is set up and covers ongoing use of BOAT, system updates and continued support.

What happens during installation and staff training? +

During setup (UGX 200,000 for businesses), we configure your accounts, Cash/MTN MoMo/Airtel Money/bank channels, approval rules and user roles. During staff training (UGX 200,000 for businesses), we coach each user on their daily screen — cashiers, bursars, storekeepers, accountants and directors — and give your team safe practice access inside BOAT Academy.

When should we add optional data management (UGX 300,000)? +

Choose optional data management if you want our team to clean, structure and import your historical Excel spreadsheets, CSV exports, Google Sheets or paper opening balances (customers owing, supplier balances, inventory items and chart-of-accounts balances). Without data management, business implementation is UGX 500,000; with full data management, it is UGX 700,000.

How is school pricing calculated for setup and monthly use? +

Schools pay a recurring subscription of UGX 1,000 per student per month. One-time school implementation is custom quoted based on enrollment, day/boarding fee structures, local LAN server needs and student balance migration — generally ranging from UGX 500,000 to UGX 3,000,000. Book a school assessment for an exact quote.

Can BOAT keep working when our internet connection drops? +

Yes. Alongside cloud web access and the lightweight BOAT Lite mobile web app, BOAT supports a Desktop Local / School LAN Server mode so bursars and cashiers on your local network can continue issuing receipts and recording entries without interruption.

Can counter staff or bursar assistants work without seeing full management accounts? +

Yes. Administrators and accountants use Full Login, while front-desk and counter staff can sign in with a restricted Staff PIN (or Member PIN where enabled). Role permissions and maker-checker approval matrices ensure staff only see and perform what their role allows.

What ongoing support do we receive after going live? +

Your monthly subscription includes ongoing technical and operational support — including guidance during month-end reconciliations, new school term rollovers, new staff onboarding and platform updates.

We already have a BOAT account — where do we sign in? +

Existing users can sign in directly at boat-frontend.vercel.app using Full Login, Member PIN or Staff PIN. This marketing website is completely separate and never alters your live workspace.
