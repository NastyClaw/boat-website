What BOAT is for

One place for the money side of your organization.

Most organizations keep finances across receipt books, spreadsheets and separate tools. BOAT replaces that patchwork with a single system that records each transaction once, routes it for approval, and turns it into the reports owners, boards and accountants need.

Record

Money in and money out, sales and invoices, supplier bills, school-fee payments, cash, bank and mobile-money accounts.

Control

Roles and permissions, amount-based approval routes, reconciliations, petty-cash controls and a month-end close with an audit trail.

Report

Trial balance, income statement, statement of financial position, cash flow, budget variance and school-fee reports.

Configuration

Staff training

Controls built in

FAQ

Common questions.

Can’t find your answer? Book a demo and ask us directly.

What does installation involve?

The BOAT team sets up your organization’s workspace and configures it — chart of accounts, departments, staff accounts and roles, approval routes and, for schools, classes, streams and fee structures. Business system setup is a one-time UGX 200,000.

How is staff training delivered?

We train your staff on the tasks they perform in BOAT. After training, BOAT Academy inside the app provides lessons matched to each person’s role, guided practice in a protected training account, and knowledge checks. Business staff training is a one-time UGX 200,000.

What is data management, and do I need it?

Data management is an optional service where we move your existing records into BOAT for you — for example customers, suppliers, items, students and opening balances — from Excel, CSV or Google Sheets. It costs a one-time UGX 300,000 for businesses. If you prefer, your team can import data using BOAT’s data migration tools instead.

What’s the difference between the implementation fee and the subscription?

The implementation fee is paid once to set up BOAT and train your staff: UGX 500,000 for businesses, or UGX 700,000 including data management. The subscription is paid every month while you use BOAT: UGX 50,000 for small businesses, UGX 100,000 for medium businesses and UGX 1,000 per student for schools.

How is school pricing calculated?

Schools pay a monthly subscription of UGX 1,000 per student — a school with 500 students pays about UGX 500,000 per month. School implementation is priced to the school’s size and enrollment, generally between UGX 500,000 and UGX 3,000,000, and confirmed after the assessment.

What support do we get after going live?

The BOAT team continues to support you after installation and training — answering questions, helping with the system and adjusting your setup as your organization changes. Support arrangements are confirmed with you during the assessment.

Do large organizations have a fixed price?

No. Large organizations and those with special requirements receive a custom quote after an assessment. Request a quote.

Can BOAT work with unreliable internet?

BOAT has a desktop local mode in which accounts are stored on the computer and internet is optional, and a server-backed mode for school networks. We’ll recommend the right setup during your assessment.

Who can see our financial information?

Access is controlled by staff roles and permissions. For example, the school dashboard shows students, fees and collections to staff, while detailed accounting remains available only to authorized roles.
