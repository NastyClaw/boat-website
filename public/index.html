Frequently asked questions.

Have a specific question about your accounts or school setup? Book a demo and ask our team directly.

001What does installation involve?

The BOAT team provisions your workspace and configures your chart of accounts, departments, staff accounts and roles, approval routes and — for schools — classes, streams and fee structures. Business system setup is a one-time UGX 200,000.

002How is staff training delivered?

We train each staff member on the exact workflows they perform in BOAT. Inside the application, BOAT Academy also provides role-matched lessons, knowledge checks and a protected practice account separate from live data. Business staff training is a one-time UGX 200,000.

003What is optional data management, and when should we add it?

Data management is an optional one-time service (UGX 300,000 for businesses) where our team prepares, imports and reconciles your existing customers, suppliers, stock items, students and opening balances from Excel, CSV or Google Sheets. Without it, business implementation is UGX 500,000; with it, UGX 700,000.

004How do one-time implementation fees differ from monthly subscriptions?

Implementation is paid once at the start to install, configure and teach BOAT to your staff. Monthly subscriptions begin once you are running and cover ongoing access, updates and support: UGX 50,000/month for small businesses, UGX 100,000/month for medium businesses and UGX 1,000 per student per month for schools.

005How is school implementation and monthly pricing determined?

Schools pay UGX 1,000 per active student per month (for example, 500 students is UGX 500,000/month). One-time school implementation depends on school size, boarding/day structure and enrollment — generally UGX 500,000 to UGX 3,000,000 — and is confirmed after assessment.

006What ongoing support is included after we go live?

Active subscribers have direct access to the BOAT support team for user assistance, permission or workflow adjustments, new term setup guidance and troubleshooting.

007How are large organizations priced?

Large organizations, multi-branch businesses and institutions with specialized approval or integration requirements receive a custom implementation and subscription quote after an operational assessment. Request a quote.

008Can BOAT run when internet connectivity is intermittent?

Yes. Alongside cloud web access and BOAT Lite, BOAT supports a desktop local mode and a local LAN server mode for school and office networks so daily cashier and bursar work continues smoothly.

009How does BOAT restrict who can view or edit financial records?

Every user is assigned a specific role (such as cashier, storekeeper, admissions officer, bursar or accountant), with maker-checker approval gates and locked accounting periods that maintain a clear audit trail.
